# Synthetic training inputs

The one-hot arrays, language-model embeddings and contact labels referenced by
`pairs.tsv` are synthetic data for checking the training workflow.

**Real RPcontact data:** [Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20971577](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20971577).
Download [source_data.zip](https://zenodo.org/api/records/20971577/files/source_data.zip/content)
for real sequences, distance matrices, dataset annotations and splits.
See [Prepare data](../README.md#prepare-data) to supply
embeddings from your chosen language models and build training inputs.
